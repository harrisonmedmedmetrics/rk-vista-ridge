# Vista Ridge: 30-Day Pilot Rollout

This is a controlled Vista Ridge lease-up pilot. It is not authority to publish, spend, launch LinkedIn, bulk-email a list, or build other property pages.

## Day 0: Launch control

The meeting must resolve the exact offer, approved property facts/media, current availability/divisibility/timing, named lead owner and backup, response SLA, verified webhook/CRM destination, production domain/indexing decision, and one read-back controlled test lead.

## Days 1–3: Launch after approval

- Promote only the approved custom-domain build.
- Enable indexing only for the approved production build.
- Re-run the full browser, gallery, accessibility, and lead-routing suite.
- Confirm the broker-ready property summary and direct inquiry URL.

Output: live measurable page, verified lead destination, first-response owner, source-of-truth scorecard.

## Days 4–7: Target accounts

- Start with approved brokers, RK relationships, nearby manufacturers, suppliers, and overflow-capacity buyers.
- Use ZoomInfo only to enrich an approved account list with relevant facilities, real-estate, operations, and supply-chain contacts.
- Document source, business rationale, owner, next action, and suppression status.

Output: human-reviewed pilot list. No bulk blast.

## Days 8–14: Activate direct and broker outreach

- Use approved, role-relevant outreach and broker introductions.
- Log every outreach, reply, qualified inquiry, tour request, and objection.
- Capture feedback on offer clarity, technical questions, availability, and page comprehension.

Output: qualified conversations, tours scheduled, and a correction list. Keep LinkedIn paused.

## Days 15–21: Convert opportunities

- Hold tours and document operational requirements.
- Share only approved facts, plans, and commercial information.
- Coordinate technical-fit and proposal steps with the correct RK owners.

Output: tour log, proposal pipeline, blockers, and a dated next step for every live opportunity.

## Days 22–30: Read the pilot

Review source quality, response-time compliance, qualification rate, tour rate, proposal rate, and deal quality. Fix the offer, facts, audience, or follow-up process before adding channels or another property.

## Day 30 decision

- Scale Vista Ridge when lead routing and SLA work and qualified inquiries, tours, or proposals show the offer is understood.
- Fix before scale when prospects are confused about the offer, availability, technical fit, or follow-up.
- Reuse the site framework for the next property only after an approved fact/media packet, verified operating loop, and documented pilot readout exist.
